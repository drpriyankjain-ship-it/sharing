What you are signing off

CDST is a mobile app for nurses (Community Health Officers at sub-centres and nurse practitioners at PHCs) in places where patients have no direct access to a doctor. The nurse records the consultation. The app drafts a triage decision, a treatment plan and a prescription. A remote doctor reviews every plan before anything is dispensed or the patient is sent home.

When the app drafts the treatment plan, it searches the national treatment guidelines listed below and bases the prescription on what they recommend. It does not use them for history taking or diagnosis. Each guideline was transcribed page by page into the app.

For each document we ask whether you approve it as a source for this purpose. Please also tell us whether you agree with the order used when two documents disagree (Q2), and whether we should add any national programme guideline (Q3).

Five sections come from the 2022 edition of the ICMR antimicrobial guidelines. They are kept only because the 2025 edition has no content on those topics, or its pages fail to load. Each will be removed once a newer edition covers it.

  2. Everything else (non-antibiotic drugs, doses, supportive care, when to refer): the ICMR Standard Treatment Workflows come first.
  3. The retained AMRTG 2022 sections are used only where AMRTG 2025 has no content on the topic.
